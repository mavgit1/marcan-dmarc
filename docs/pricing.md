The two operator plans differ only in the number of client domains. Prices are in USD; checkout may show the amount in your local currency. No VAT is charged.

Questions, or a license for something these plans don't fit: [marcan@kaitiaki.tech](mailto:marcan@kaitiaki.tech). The same address is the place to ask if you would like Marcan hosted for you, or help setting it up.

## What a license changes

- Your name as the sender: "Acme IT", not "Acme IT via Marcan".
- Your letterhead alone at the top of the email, without Marcan's line first.
- Your footer: the emails end in "Sent by Acme IT."
- No free-software notice in client reports and alerts, nor under "Marcan" in the sidebar.

Nothing else changes: every feature is in the free software. The details, with examples, are under [What a key changes](./license#what-a-key-changes).

## Good to know

- **Trial.** 30 days on both plans. It ends into the subscription unless you cancel during it; then nothing is charged.
- **Cancelling.** Any time, in the [Stripe customer portal](https://billing.stripe.com/p/login/3cIdRa2g90ar77I1vdeEo01), also linked on **Settings → License** while a key is stored. When a license ends, the duty to tell your clients comes back; see [When a license ends](./license#when-a-license-ends).
- **The key.** It arrives by email. Enter it under **Settings → License → Operator key** and save; it is checked inside Marcan, never online. See [The operator key and the notice](./license#the-operator-key-and-the-notice).
- **Instances.** One license covers all instances one operator runs, test instances included.
- **Client domains.** Only client domains count, as registered domains: `example.com` and `mail.example.com` are one. Your own domains and those of your group companies never count. Who counts as a client is under [Domains of clients](./license#domains-of-clients-tell-them-or-buy-a-license).

A license covers running Marcan for your clients. Passing Marcan on to others is always under the Beneficiary Copyleft License, and the license itself cannot be transferred or sublicensed. Buying a license means agreeing to the [Marcan Operator License v1](/licenses/operator-v1).
